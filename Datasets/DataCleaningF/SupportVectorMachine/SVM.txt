OOOOO----|-----XXXXXXX Focusing on the observations on the edges of each cluster, we can chose the midpoint between them 
as threshold.
The shortest distance between the observations and the threshold is called MARGIN.
When we use the threshold that gives the maximum margin we use the maximum margin classifier.
But these classifiers are super sensitive to outliers, because a left side element could move the margin really cloase to the right side.
OOOOO-------O-|-XXXXXXX
Any new classification which is more close to the right group than it is to the left group would be 
put in the left group even if it would fit better to the right group.

OOOOO-|-X-------XXXXXXX In this case the new observation close to the boundary would be classified in the right group, but
it is more close to the left one.


So, to make a threshold that is not  so sensitive to outliers we must allow misclassification

OOOOO-----|X-O--XXXXXXX

Choosing a threshold that allows misclassification is an example of the bias/variance tradeoff that plagues machine learning.
If we do not allow misclassification we  are costrained to chose a threshold very sensitive to training data,LOW BIAS
AND it performs poorly on new data HIGH VARIANCE
iN THIS CASE
OOOOO-----|X-O--XXXXXXX
the threshold is less sensitive to training data, and allows misclassification  HIGHER BIAS.
It performas better on new data LOW  VARIANCE

When misclassification is allowed, the distance between observations and threshold is called soft margin.
